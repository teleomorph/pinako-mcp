## What's new

- **Updating keeps your connections.** When the AI Bridge is already on your computer, the installer refreshes the AI apps you have connected to Pinako and leaves the others alone. To add an app, press Connect more apps on the last screen. A first install works as before.
- **Your AI apps' own settings are kept.** An update changes only what an app needs to reach Pinako. If an app's settings file cannot be read, the installer leaves it as it is and tells you.
- **The AI Bridge now tells Pinako which version it is.** When a future Pinako needs a newer AI Bridge, Pinako can say so and give you a button to download it. Pinako 1.3.25 and later use this.
- **Updating is more reliable.** The installer can now replace the AI Bridge while your browser and AI apps are open. If it cannot, it says so and lets you try again.

To update, download the file for your computer below and run it. Pinako reconnects by itself in about a minute.
