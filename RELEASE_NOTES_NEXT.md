## What's new

- **If you use Pinako in more than one browser, AI clients no longer lose track of one of them.** Each browser runs its own Bridge, and one of those Bridges answers the AI apps for all of them. When that role passed from one Bridge to another (for example, after you restarted a browser), another browser could drop out of view: AI clients could not see or edit it until you happened to change one of its tabs. It now reconnects on its own.
- **Your Pinako data now needs the access key to be read, not only to be changed.** The installer has written a private access key into every AI app's settings since v1.2.0, but an app (or any program on the computer) without it could still read your tabs, libraries, bookmarks and notes. Now it gets nothing: every tool answers with a message asking you to re-run the Pinako AI Bridge installer, which refreshes the app's settings. Apps the installer set up already have the key, so most people will notice nothing.
- **Clear message when two people share one computer.** Every signed-in user account on a computer shares Pinako's connection, so while one person's Pinako runs, another person's AI apps reach it and are refused. They now say so plainly (another user account is using Pinako's connection) instead of reporting that the Bridge is not running or asking to sign in.
